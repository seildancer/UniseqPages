Welcome to Uniseq

Uniseq is a local-first outliner and personal knowledge management (PKM) desktop app. Write in plain Markdown — your files are always real files on your machine, never locked in a proprietary database.

We believe your notes should belong to you. By default, everything lives in Markdown files you can read, edit, move, or back up with any tool you already use — nothing leaves your machine. The optional Uniseq Sync feature lets you sync across devices via our backend if you want it.

File-First

Every page is a plain .md file. Open them in any editor, sync them with any tool — Uniseq never holds your data hostage.

Outliner + Streams

Structure your thinking with hierarchical outliner pages and date-stamped stream journals for daily capture.

Wikilink References

Link pages together with [[Page]] syntax. Uniseq indexes all references so you can navigate your knowledge graph instantly.

Flexible Sync

Stay local, use the built-in Uniseq Sync to store notes on our backend, or plug in your own backend via the custom sync feature. You choose.

How It Works

1. Open any folder on your computer as a workspace

2. Create and organize pages in an outliner tree

3. Capture daily notes in stream journals

4. Navigate connections via [[wikilinks]] and search

Your Data, Your Control

Uniseq collects no telemetry and requires no account for local use. All content is stored as Markdown files in the folder you choose, and the app works fully offline. If you opt in to Uniseq Sync, your notes are stored on our backend and you can delete them — or your entire account — at any time.
